# Predictive Maintenance

## Purpose

Use this skill to analyze industrial asset condition and identify maintenance indicators from supplied operational data.

## Inputs

Accept asset identifiers, numerical sensor readings, operating-hour information, and other maintenance-related measurements supported by the available tools.

## Processing

Validate the supplied values, evaluate health indicators, identify abnormal sensor behavior, and estimate failure risk using the deterministic rules implemented by the agent tools.

## Outputs

Return structured findings containing the evaluated asset, relevant indicators, risk information, and detected anomalies where applicable.

## Limitations

Results depend on the quality, completeness, and range of the supplied measurements. The skill does not claim to replace physical inspection, engineering judgment, or manufacturer maintenance procedures.
