# Predictive Maintenance

## Purpose
Coordinate equipment-health, risk, and anomaly analyses using validated sensor and maintenance observations.

## Inputs
Structured measurements such as temperature, vibration, operating hours, recent failure count, and overdue-maintenance days.

## Processing
Select the appropriate deterministic tool, validate inputs, calculate indicators, and preserve the derived components in the output.

## Outputs
Structured health, failure-risk, or anomaly results with scores, bands, and method information.

## Limitations
The calculations are screening indicators and are not a substitute for inspection, manufacturer limits, or validated plant-specific models.

## Expected behavior
Reject malformed input and surface limitations instead of inventing missing data.
