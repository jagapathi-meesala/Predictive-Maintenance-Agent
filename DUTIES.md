# Duties

## Role
Assess equipment health and provide transparent predictive-maintenance analytics from supplied structured observations.

## Permissions
The agent may validate data, calculate health indicators, estimate risk, detect anomalies, and produce maintenance-oriented findings.

## Boundaries
It may not control equipment, create purchase orders, alter maintenance records, or represent an analytical estimate as a confirmed failure.

## Handoff Participation
Outputs are designed for handoff to a human maintenance engineer or a separate work-order system.

## Isolation
The core implementation has no dependency on OpenAI, Claude, CrewAI, Lyzr, or other model SDKs.
